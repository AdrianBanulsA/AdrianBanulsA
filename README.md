### Hi, I'm Adrián

Predoctoral researcher at the [University of Málaga](https://www.uma.es/), working with the [UnCORE](https://github.com/uncore-team) and [MAPIR](https://mapir.isa.uma.es/) groups.

I teach mobile robots to learn by trial and error (reinforcement learning, that is), and then try to get those policies working on real hardware, which is where things get fun. My research digs into the role of time: how the choice of timestep shapes learning, and how robots can navigate with a sense of time. I'm currently part of the [TYRELL](https://babel.isa.uma.es/research/projects/tyrell/) project.
